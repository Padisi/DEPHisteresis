Trap

The DEP depth on the 3D grid of the Fokker–Planck runs, per unit $\mathrm{Re}\,K$, is tabulated against the screening parameter $\Omega = 2\pi f\,\Lambda\,\varepsilon_m\,\ell/(\sigma_m\lambda_D)$ and rescaled: $U = -\mathrm{Re}\,K(f)\,(V_0/1\,\mathrm V)^2\,(b/55\,\mathrm{nm})^3\,(\varepsilon_m/78.5)\,u(\mathbf r;\Omega)$. From it come the capacity of the ROI, $I_{eq}(f)$ (packing-limited Boltzmann, $P = P_{max}/[1 + (P_{max}/P_{res}-1)e^{U}]$), and the trap depth $U_{max} = \max|U|$.

Dynamics

There are two compartments, in units of the bulk ROI content. $I$ is the ROI, which is measured. $J$ is the near field around the electrodes, refilled from the bulk at rate $k_b = R/J_0$ with $R = 4Da_{eff}/V_{ROI}$:

$$\frac{dI}{dt} = \begin{cases} (k_D J/I_{eq})\,(I_{eq} - I), & I < I_{eq}\\ -k_{out}\,(I - I_{eq}), & I > I_{eq}\end{cases} \qquad k_{out} = \begin{cases} k_D\,e^{-\beta U_{max}}, & \mathrm{Re}\,K > 0\\ k_D\,(1 + U_{max}), & \mathrm{Re}\,K < 0\end{cases}$$ $$\frac{dJ}{dt} = k_b\,(J_0 - J) - \frac{dI}{dt},$$

Here $k_D = D/l^2$. Under nDEP, $J$ relaxes toward $J_0 e^{-\gamma U_{max}}$. The toy internals $J_0 = 42.9$, $\beta = 0.602$, $\gamma = 0.238$, $a_{eff} = 10\,\mu$m and $l = 0.8\,\mu$m are calibrated on the 3D runs and are not on the sliders.

Measured signal

The out-of-focus column adds a constant background, so $I_{Fluo} = (I + H)/(1 + H)$, divided by its maximum in each sweep. The first plot shows it at the end of each 2 s step. The second shows Re K and the trap depth $U_{DEP} = \pm\max|U|$ in $k_BT$ (+ for pDEP, − for nDEP, on a symmetric log axis). The third shows the number of particles in the focus-weighted ROI over the two staircases, $N = I\,c_\infty V_{ROI}$, with $V_{ROI} \approx 25\,\mu\mathrm m^3$ ($N \approx 2.5$ with no trap).
